# Castle Chaos v1.8 — Balanced Comeback

- Placement awards: 30 / 20 / 15 / 10 Glory (4th–8th share 10). Equal skill scores share the same rank award.
- Catch-up is based on overall Glory standings **before** each mini-game: with two connected players the trailing player gets +10; with 3–8 players, ranks 3–4 get +5, 5–6 get +10, and 7–8 get +15. Equal Glory shares standing and the leader gets no bonus.
- A player must earn a positive skill score to receive a comeback bonus. Disconnected players receive nothing. An all-zero round awards only +5 participation.
- In stage 10, placement rewards are doubled; comeback bonuses are not doubled. Crowns retain their 50-point finale value.
- Results screen displays base placement, finale multiplier, comeback bonus, and new Glory total.
- New automated 10-stage comeback integration test plus focused multi-player scoring checks.

**Note:** The game still trusts client-submitted mini-game skill scores subject to server limits. This is not cheat-proof.
